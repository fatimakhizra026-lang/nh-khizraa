Our Heritage

The Story of NH KHIZRA FATIMA

Our Story

Born from a deep reverence for Pakistani craftsmanship, NH KHIZRA FATIMA is a luxury fashion house that celebrates the timeless elegance of Eastern wear. Founded with a vision to merge heritage with modernity, every piece we create is a tribute to the artisans, weavers, and embroiderers who have preserved our cultural legacy for generations.

Our Vision

To redefine Pakistani fashion on the global stage — offering designs that are sophisticated, feminine, and unapologetically luxurious. We believe clothing is not just fabric; it is an expression of identity, grace, and confidence.

Our Craft

Each garment begins with the finest handpicked fabrics — pure silk from Sindh, chiffon from Faisalabad, lawn from the mills of Punjab. Our master karigars spend hundreds of hours on intricate embroidery, zardozi, tilla, and sequin work, ensuring every stitch tells a story.

Our Promise

Quality without compromise. Elegance without excess. Timeless design that transcends seasons. When you wear NH KHIZRA FATIMA, you wear a legacy.
